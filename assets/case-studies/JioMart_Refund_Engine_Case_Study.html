The Problem

Refund operations were straining under fragmentation

JioMart's payment ecosystem was straining under fragmented, manual refund operations. Refunds were routed through gateway-specific logic with no central system, and every case still needed a human to verify, track, and approve it.

01 · SLOW TURNAROUND

Refunds took too long to reach customers

Refunds routed through fragmented, gateway-specific logic took too long to reach customers, steadily eroding trust.

02 · HEAVY MANUAL EFFORT

No central system or visibility

Ops teams manually verified, tracked, and approved refund cases with no central system or visibility into status.

03 · RISING SUPPORT LOAD

Support volume absorbed the fallout

Payment and order-mismatch queries became a disproportionate share of total customer support volume.

Diagnosis

Four root causes

Digging into the refund journey surfaced four distinct, fixable causes — each became a workstream toward faster, more accurate refunds.

01

Gaps in the Post-Order State Diagram

Undefined transitions let orders drift into states with no clear next action.

02

No Visibility into Unusual Approvals

Anomalous approvals went unnoticed, and every case was actioned one by one.

03

Orders Without a Generated Order ID

Some orders left no system record, forcing refunds entirely outside the standard flow.

04

Missing or Short Shipments

Cases like "10 ordered, 9 received" relied entirely on ad hoc, manual verification.

Solutions 3 & 4

Structured exception handling

Replaced ad hoc handling with dedicated, trackable flows for the two hardest edge cases.

Missing Order ID

1

Customer reports the issue in-app.

2

Structured claim questionnaire completed — in-app form captures order & payment details.

3

Warehouse verifies and approves.

4

Admin sign-off, then refund issued.

Missing / Short Shipment

1

Customer uploads photo evidence.

2

Case logged in a dedicated, tracked queue.

3

Refund processed on approval.

4

 

Both flows feed the same Bulk Approval Portal, so cases sourced manually can still be fast-tracked at scale.

Looking Ahead

Risks & mitigations

What could undermine these fixes — and how the design accounts for it.

Alert Fatigue on the Dashboard

Poorly tuned thresholds could bury real issues in noise — mitigated with ongoing calibration.

Rubber-Stamping in Bulk Approvals

Speed shouldn't cost rigor — paired with sampling-based audits on bulk-cleared cases.

Gaming Image-Based Claims

Photo evidence alone can be manipulated — mitigated by requiring independent warehouse verification.

Backlog in the No-Order-ID Queue

A separately tracked queue can itself bottleneck without clear SLAs and ownership.
