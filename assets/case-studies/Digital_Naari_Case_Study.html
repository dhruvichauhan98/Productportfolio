The Problem

Formal financial products weren't designed for her

Women make up roughly 10% of India's Business Correspondent agents, and formal product adoption among women customers — savings, credit, insurance, investments — stayed low despite women holding 55.6% of all PMJDY accounts. Existing products assumed steady income, easy bank access, and comfort transacting with male agents — none of which matched last-mile women's reality.

01 · RIGID SAVINGS PRODUCTS

Formal savings demanded large, fixed deposits

Women saved informally through gold, chit funds, and SHGs because formal products didn't flex to irregular, small-ticket income.

02 · TRUST & DELIVERY GAPS

E-commerce and investing stayed out of reach

5–7 km delivery gaps and low digital confidence kept women from acting on demand they clearly had for both commerce and formal investing.

03 · MALE-DEFAULT DISTRIBUTION

Only ~10% of BC agents were women

Female customers transact up to 66% higher values with female agents, yet the agent network wasn't built to reflect that preference.

Diagnosis

Four insights from the field

Research across focus group discussions, a 634-respondent survey, and industry data surfaced four behavior patterns — each became a direct input into product design.

01

Savings Behavior

Women save informally, mainly toward children's education, weddings, and emergencies — not wealth accumulation.

02

Loan Behavior

Loans of ₹25K–₹1L are common, but repayment responsibility falls on women even when husbands direct the borrowing.

03

Insurance & Investment Behavior

Products like SIPs and PMSBY are known but avoided — seen as risky or illiquid, with no trusted person nearby to explain them.

04

Commerce Behavior

Women want the variety e-commerce offers but are blocked by delivery distance and low confidence navigating apps alone.

Looking Ahead

Risks & mitigations

What could undermine these fixes — and how the design accounts for it.

Agent Capacity Bottleneck

Scaling faster than agent training could create service-quality gaps — mitigated with phased onboarding and refresher training.

Trust Builds Slower for Investment Products

Insurance and mutual fund trust moves slower than commerce adoption — mitigated with longer awareness runways and simplified messaging.

Logistics Outside Product Control

Delivery-distance barriers can't be solved by the app alone — mitigated through parallel logistics partnerships.

Documentation Still Excludes Some Women

Aadhaar-related issues, especially for minors, continue to limit account access — mitigated by simplified-KYC advocacy.
