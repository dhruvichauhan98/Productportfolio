About

Curious about why, before how.

Why Product Management

It's the one career where my curiosity feels completely at home. I'd rather understand why something works than just that it does — talking to customers, questioning assumptions, connecting perspectives before touching a solution. It sits at the intersection of business, technology and human behaviour, and my favourite wins are quiet ones: a simpler journey, one less friction point, value created on both sides.

For me, this was never a career choice — it's how I already think. Six years building and scaling FinTech, AI, eCommerce, CRM and MarTech products, and now, at IIM Kozhikode, sharpening the strategic and leadership perspective needed to build products that create lasting impact.
